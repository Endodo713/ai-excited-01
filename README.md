Entry for [make.fun's ai-excited-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) — one image, made to excite the AI judge: a colossal purple octopus bursting
out of a small white bathtub in a cozy tiled bathroom, water exploding in
every direction, shampoo bottles and a rubber duck flying through the air,
and a boy in a towel staring up in shock in the doorway. Warm golden evening
light through the window; the octopus is huge and central, so the idea reads
at a glance even small.

`main.py` copies the image (`entry.png`, held in this repository) to
`/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, deterministically.

Run it:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
